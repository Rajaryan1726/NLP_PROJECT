Bharat sarkar ne aaj ek naya digital education scheme launch kiya hai jiska naam "Vidya Setu" hai. Is scheme ke tahat 2 crore students ko free tablets diye jayenge agle do saal mein. Education Minister ne press conference mein bataya ki ye scheme especially rural areas ke liye design kiya gaya hai jaha internet connectivity ek challenge rahi hai. Scheme ka total budget 4,500 crore rupaye rakha gaya hai.
