The Reserve Bank of India kept the repo rate unchanged at 6.5% on Friday, the eighth consecutive time it has held rates. Governor Shaktikanta Das said inflation had eased to 4.8% in May but food prices remained a concern. The central bank raised its GDP growth forecast for 2024-25 from 7% to 7.2%. Four of the six members of the Monetary Policy Committee voted to keep the rate unchanged.
