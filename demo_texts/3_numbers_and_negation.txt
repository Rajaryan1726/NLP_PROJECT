Mumbai mein kal raat 212 mm baarish hui, jo is season ki sabse zyada hai. BMC ne bataya ki 14 local trains cancel ki gayi, lekin Western Railway ki services band nahi hui. School aur colleges 24 July ko bandh rahenge, par offices khule rahenge. IMD ne agle 48 ghante ke liye orange alert jaari kiya hai, red alert nahi. Andheri subway mein 3 feet paani bhara tha aur use subah 6 baje tak band rakha gaya.
